Journal article · AI in Healthcare

AI-Powered Early Detection of Cardiovascular Diseases: A Global Health Priority

M. S. Islam, M. M. Uddin, M. F. Hossain, M. R. Hussain, O. A. Sarker, T. R. Tonmoy, R. K. Shorna

Journal of Advances in Medical Sciences and Artificial Intelligence, 1(1), pp. 19–25. C5K Research Publication.

Studies whether combining several data sources improves early detection of cardiovascular disease. Gradient-boosting models for structured health-record and wearable data are combined with convolutional neural networks for cardiac imaging in an ensemble, and compared against single-source models using precision, recall, F1 and AUC-ROC. The paper reports that the integrated multimodal model outperforms record-only and imaging-only models, and discusses privacy (HIPAA/GDPR), algorithmic bias, explainability and deployment in low-resource settings.

My contribution: literature review on multimodal clinical AI and single-source diagnostic limitations, and support with drafting and revising the manuscript.

2024
Journal article · Security & Blockchain

Blockchain Based Security Solutions for Banking Information Technology

S. N. Hasan, M. K. K. Shinfa, O. A. Sarker, M. R. Hussain, J. T. Meraj

Transactions on Banking, Finance, and Leadership Informatics, 1(1), pp. 25–29. C5K Research Publication.

Examines how blockchain can upgrade banks' payment-clearing and credit-information systems. It covers encrypted, user-owned credit data, KYC information sharing across institutions without duplicated work, and the trade-offs between public, consortium and private blockchains for transaction speed and security. It argues for regulatory sandboxes and industry standards as preconditions for adoption.

My contribution: review of blockchain use cases for secure information sharing (credit data and KYC) and the comparison of blockchain types, plus manuscript editing.

Review article · IoT & Data

Precision Farming Through the Use of Internet of Things (IoT) Innovations in Agriculture

M. R. Hussain, J. T. Meraj, O. A. Sarker

Journal of Sustainable Agricultural Economics, 1(1), pp. 18–21. C5K Research Publication.

Reviews IoT and big-data approaches to precision agriculture: sensing and data acquisition with ground sensors, drones and satellites; decision-making with variable-rate technologies and smart irrigation; edge computing for real-time analytics; and the cost, infrastructure and data-management barriers to adoption.

My contribution: survey of IoT architectures and edge-computing approaches for agricultural monitoring, and writing of the related review sections.

Undergraduate thesis

Decentralized Social Network Using Blockchain

O. A. Sarker · B.Sc. thesis, Department of CSE, Daffodil International University

Explores a social-network design in which user identity and content are not controlled by a single central platform. The thesis uses blockchain to give users ownership of their data and to keep records tamper-evident, and examines the trade-offs this brings for performance and usability.
